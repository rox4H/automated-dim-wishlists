# Contribute

## Suggest a roll

Open a [recommendation issue](https://github.com/rox4H/destiny-2-weapon-database/issues/new?template=recommendation-proposal.yml). Submit one PvE or PvP roll per issue. Fill in the mode, name, description, exact weapon version ID (for example, `avalanche_s5`), and four perks.

## Report a mistake

Open a [correction issue](https://github.com/rox4H/destiny-2-weapon-database/issues/new?template=data-correction.yml). Identify the weapon and describe the mistake.

Do not include private information.
